## REQUIRED CORRECTION B

Organization Resolution Middleware

Current Design:

RequireTenantRole()

resolves organization from route parameter.

Problem:

Many routes do not contain orgId.

Examples:

/submissions/:id

/groups/:id

/certificates/:certNumber

---

Required:

Resource Organization Resolver

Flow

submissionId

↓

submission

↓

organizationId

↓

membership validation

---

Do not rely only on route parameters.
