3. Never skip payment verification.
4. Never allow cross-organization access.
5. Never generate duplicate certificates.
6. Never hard delete critical records.
7. Always maintain audit logs.
8. Always paginate large datasets.
9. Always use indexes.
10. Design every feature for future scale.
